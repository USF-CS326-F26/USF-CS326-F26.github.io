# Week 9 · Watch First

Four short videos for after the Oct 13 lecture and before Thursday's and
Friday's sessions (Oct 22 and Oct 23): about 20 minutes in all. None shows
solution code: no Rust, assembly or pseudocode for anything you write, only the
machine's state, the given code, and what each line of test output means. Their
numbers are examples, not the lecture's.

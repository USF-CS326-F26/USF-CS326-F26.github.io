# Midterm 2, Section 02, Problem 3: Inodes and Directories

**Midterm 2, Section 02**, problem 3 of 5 · **Length:** 12:19 · **Exercise:** `40k_filesystem` (Week 11) · **Exam:** [Section 02](../assignments/midterm-2-section-02.pdf) (PDF) · **Answer key:** [Solutions](../assignments/midterm-2-section-02-solutions.pdf) (PDF)

Three parts, worked on pages 6 and 7: (a) what nine `dircreate` and `dirlookup` calls return right after `fs.init()`, in a table; (b) a longer `write`, a shorter `write` and a `read`: what each returns, the file's size, the bytes read, and why the old bytes do not come back; (c) a `dirlookup` that compares all `NAMELEN` bytes of the stored name: what four calls return, and what goes wrong. The answer key's handwriting is written onto the exam page as each step is worked.

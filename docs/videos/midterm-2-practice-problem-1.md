# Midterm 2 Practice Exam, Problem 1: Processes and Scheduling

**Midterm 2 practice exam**, problem 1 of 6 · **Length:** 9:17 · **Exercises:** `34k_processes` (Week 9) · `36k_scheduling` (Week 9) · **Problems:** [Practice exam](../assignments/midterm-2-practice.pdf) (PDF) · **Answer key:** [Solutions](../assignments/midterm-2-practice-solutions.pdf) (PDF)

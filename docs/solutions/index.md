# Solutions

Exam and practice-set solutions are posted here after each is reviewed in
class.

**Exercise solutions are not published.** They live only in the instructor
repository. You unlock the reference solution for an individual exercise on
your own machine once you have passed it:

```sh
oslings solution 03_paging
```

That is deliberate. The exercises are the course, and an exercise whose answer
is a click away is not an exercise. Once you have passed one, reading the
reference version is genuinely worthwhile — it is often shorter than what you
wrote, and the difference is the lesson.

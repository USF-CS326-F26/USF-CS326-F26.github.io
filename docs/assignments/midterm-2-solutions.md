# Midterm 2 Solutions

Midterm 2 was given on Thursday, November 19, in two versions, one for each
section. Both ask the same five problems with different inputs, addresses and
names, and the options in a different order. A few parts are set up
differently: in 1(a) a process goes to sleep in Section 01 and wakes up in
Section 02; 2(d) leaves `PTE_W` off the UART's page in Section 01 and off RAM in
Section 02; and 5(b) traces `getpid` in Section 01 and `write` in Section 02.
Each version is posted here, blank and with its key, and a video for each
problem works the key on that exam's own pages.

Work the blank exam on paper before you open the key or a video. Reading a
solution feels like learning and mostly is not.
